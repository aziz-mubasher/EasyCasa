# Demo script — 10 minutes (EC-15)

Italian-only for now. Host: `https://demo.easycasaita.com` (banner always visible).

Seed refs (stable across `demo:seed` / `demo:reset`):

| Step | Ref / data |
|------|------------|
| 2 | Listing `DEMO-SC1-VERIFIED` / slug `demo-sc1-verified` |
| 3 | Admin draft `DEMO-SC2-BLOCKED` |
| 4 | Enquiry seeker `demo-seeker-badged@…` · band €325k · initials `M.R.` · no phone |
| 5 | Enquiry `demo-seeker-plain@…` · no B4A columns |
| 6 | Enquiry `demo-seeker-expired@…` · expiry `2025-01-01` (hidden) |
| 6b | Viewing CONFIRMED · seeker `Giulia Bianchi` · listing address on SC1 |
| 7 | Coverage: Cremona `APE_ISSUANCE` demand ×11; Milan two CENED pros |
| Admin | Takedown on SC3 (`removed` + motivation); open report on SC2; DSAR `demo-dsar@…`; credential `CENED-EXP-12D` (+12d) |

1. **Search.** Map, listings, energy class on every card.  
   *“Every property here has an identity-verified owner. That's a precondition of publishing, not a badge.”*

2. **Listing detail — OMI band beside asking price** (`DEMO-SC1-VERIFIED`).  
   *“That's the official Agenzia delle Entrate range for this specific microzone. The asking price sits below it — and we tell the buyer to find out why.”*

3. **Admin: blocked listing** (`DEMO-SC2-BLOCKED`).  
   *“Here's what didn't get published, and the reason.”*

4. **Enquiry from a badged buyer.**  
   *“The owner knows this person can fund it to €325,000. They don't know their surname, and they don't have their phone number.”*

5. **Enquiry with no badge.**  
   *“Nothing. We don't flag people for not using it.”*

6. **Confirm the viewing** (seeded `CONFIRMED` on SC1).  
   *“Now both sides get the name and the address. At the same moment.”*

7. **Admin: coverage matrix** (Cremona demand ×11 vs Milan certifiers ×2).  
   *“Milan is covered. Cremona isn't, and eleven people have asked.”*

8. **Valutazione, logged out.**  
   *“No account, no phone number, no callback — same official data.”*

Rehearse steps 4–6: capacity visible, identity invisible, until both choose.
