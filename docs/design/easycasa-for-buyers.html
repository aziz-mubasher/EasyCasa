Why you can trust what you see

Genuine homes, genuine owners

A commission-free marketplace only works if every listing is real. So we make sellers earn their place on it.

Identity-verified sellers

Private sellers verify who they are before they can publish. Verified owners are marked on every listing.

Ownership checks

Listings carry cadastral identifiers, and ownership documentation is checked against the seller's verified identity.

Price-anomaly screening

Listings priced far outside their official OMI zone range are flagged and reviewed — bait pricing doesn't survive here.

One-tap reporting
