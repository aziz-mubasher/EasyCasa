# Mediation & Provvigione Disclosure (portal framing — EC-S Claim 2 reconcile)

> Reviewed with AZM product-owner authorisation (2026-08-13 Claim 1–2 flip).
> Aligns with T04 matrix (portal / no *mediazione* for private-seller hosting) and
> sell-privately Claim 2 copy. Not a substitute for external counsel advice.

**EasyCasa role on sell-privately / owner listings:** hosting **portale** — we publish
listings and provide neutral tools (enquiries, scheduling, optional flat-fee
services). We do **not** act as *mediatore* under L. 39/1989 for those listings:
we do not put parties in relation to conclude the deal, negotiate terms, or take a
**provvigione** / percentage of the sale price.

**Shown at the enquiry step (short form):**

> EasyCasa opera come portale di pubblicazione annunci: non svolge attività di
> mediazione immobiliare su questa richiesta e non matura una **provvigione** sul
> prezzo di vendita. Eventuali servizi opzionali (es. boost, premium, elenco
> professionisti a tariffa fissa) hanno un prezzo separato, indicato prima
> dell'ordine, indipendente dall'esito della vendita. Inviando la richiesta
> accetti l'[Informativa privacy] e prendi visione di questa disclosure.

**Why gated:** records that the enquirer saw the portal / no-*provvigione*
framing (consent purpose `mediation_disclosure`, versioned for audit). The
purpose key name is historical; the content is portal disclosure, not a
mediation mandate.

**Optional paid services:** flat-fee only (T04 row 8). Never success-contingent
or listing-price percentage.

**Not covered here:** third-party professionals (notaio, etc.) you contact
directly via the informational / paid-placement partner directory; their fees
are theirs. Rows 10–12 of the T04 matrix (offers, *proposta*, negotiation
advice) remain prohibited for EasyCasa until a separate S5 counsel path.
